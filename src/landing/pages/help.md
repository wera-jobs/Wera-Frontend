## Getting started

### What does Wera cost?

Nothing. Wera is free during the pilot. If we ever charge, we will tell you the price first, and you can choose not to continue.

### How do I set up my profile?

Sign up, then follow the three steps: upload your resume or skip it, choose the roles, levels and places you want, and review the profile Wera drafts for you. It takes about three minutes, and you can change any of it later in Profile.

### Do I need a resume?

No, but it helps. Without one, describe yourself in the second step and Wera drafts your profile from your answers.

## Matches and scores

### Where do the jobs come from?

From the public career pages of more than 5,000 companies, on job boards such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday. Wera checks them several times a day.

### Why do some jobs have no score?

Wera ranks every job that passes your filters, then scores the best ones with AI each day, best first. The rest stay ranked, and more scores fill in as you browse.

### What does the fit score mean?

A number from 0 to 100 for how well a job fits your profile, with the reasons: the skills you have, the ones the job asks for, seniority, location and sponsorship. It only orders jobs for you. It is never sent to employers.

### Why can't I find a job I expected?

Your filters may have dropped it: the levels, places, years of experience and work authorization in your preferences. Every job your filters drop is listed on the Excluded page, with the reason.

## Sponsorship

### How does Wera know who sponsors?

In two ways. It reads what each posting says about visas, and it looks up the employer in the US Department of Labor's public H-1B filings: how often it files, for which titles and at what pay.

### Does a past filing mean they will sponsor me?

No. A filing shows what an employer has done before, and it is not a promise. Ask the employer, and talk to an immigration attorney or your school's international office about your situation. Nothing on Wera is legal advice.

## Resumes and applications

### How does tailoring work?

Wera reorders your skills and rewords your bullets for one posting. It can only use what is already on your resume, so it never adds a new role, number or skill. Check the result before you send it.

### Can I download my resume?

Yes, as a PDF or as LaTeX, in any of four designs.

### How do I track my applications?

Save a job and it appears on the tracker. Drag it across Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer and Rejected, or use the status menu on the card. To download your tracked jobs as a CSV file, go to Settings.

## The Chrome extension

### How do I get the extension?

It is in a private pilot and coming to the Chrome Web Store. To join the pilot, write to us.

### Does Wera apply for me?

No. The extension fills in the form for you to check. You review every answer and press submit yourself.

## Your account

### I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the log-in page. If you don't see it, email is not switched on yet: write to us from the address on your account and we will help.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and choose Delete account. Your profile, matches, tracker, letters and files are removed. The [privacy policy](/privacy) has the details.
