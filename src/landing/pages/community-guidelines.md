The Wera community is for offers, interview experiences and tips, from people in the same search. These rules keep it useful and kind.

## What belongs here

- Honest accounts of offers, interviews and rejections
- What worked, what did not, and what you would do differently
- Questions and answers about the job search
- Sponsorship experiences and how you navigated them

## What does not

- Harassment, hate or threats, or attacks on people for their background, nationality or immigration status
- Cheating, including leaked assessment questions, and faked credentials
- Spam, referral selling, paid promotion or recruiting
- Other people's private information, or confidential details from an employer
- Advice presented as legal or immigration fact. Share your experience and suggest a professional
- Off-topic content

## How moderation works

Every post and comment is reviewed by an AI moderator before it goes live. If it is refused, you are told why, and you can edit it and try again. Automated review makes mistakes, so people on our team can publish what was hidden in error, and hide or delete what got through. If moderation cannot run, nothing is published until it can.

## Be careful with what you share

Posts are visible to all Wera users. Leave out anything that identifies you if you would not want an employer to read it.
