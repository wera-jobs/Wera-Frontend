Wera is Swahili slang for a job. It is also a service that does the slow part of a job search for you.

Several times a day, Wera checks the career pages of more than 5,000 companies, from AI labs and banks to hospitals and schools. It drops the jobs you cannot apply to, ranks the rest against your resume, and scores your best matches with the reasons. For each employer, it shows what the posting says about visas and what the company has filed with the US Department of Labor.

When a job is worth applying to, Wera helps you get the application right: a resume tailored to that posting, a cover letter, practice questions for the interview, and a Chrome extension that fills in the form while you check every answer.

## Who it is for

Wera is built for students, new grads and international talent, who often have the least time and the most at stake. It works for any career with jobs posted online, from software engineering to nursing.

## How we work

- Every score comes with its reasons, so you can see why a job was put in front of you.
- Wera never submits an application for you. You press submit.
- We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
- You can delete your account and your data at any time, in Settings.

## Where we are

Wera is in a free pilot. Some features, like the Chrome extension, are open only to pilot users for now, and the service can be briefly unavailable while we work on it. We add companies and fix problems every week.
