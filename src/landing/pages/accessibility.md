We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), level AA.

## What we do

- Keyboard access throughout the app, with a visible focus outline
- Light and dark themes, with small text at 4.5:1 contrast or better
- Motion that follows your device's reduced motion setting
- Layouts that work on phones and at high zoom
- Labels on form fields, and text alternatives for images

## Known limits

We have not had an independent audit. The job tracker's drag and drop has a status menu on every card as an alternative. Job postings and resumes come from others, and we cannot guarantee their accessibility.
