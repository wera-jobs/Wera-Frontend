AI does much of the work in Wera. Here is where, and exactly what it sees.

## Where AI is used

| Feature | What AI does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Job facts | Reads each posting for seniority, skills, work mode and sponsorship | The posting only |
| Fit score | Rates how well a job fits you, with a reason | A short summary of the job and your profile text |
| Deep review | Takes a closer look at a few of your top matches | The posting and your profile text |
| Setup suggestions | Pre-fills roles, levels and places when you sign up | Your resume |
| Profile draft | Drafts your profile for you to review | Your resume and questionnaire answers |
| Resume import, tailoring and suggestions | Converts a resume, tailors a copy, and rewrites a bullet | Your resume, and the job if there is one |
| Cover letters | Writes a letter for one job | Your profile, your resume and the job |
| Extension answers | Suggests answers to form fields and drafts open questions | The question, your profile and the job |
| Reading a page | Turns a job page into a job when you ask | The text a person can see on the page |
| Moderation | Reviews community posts and comments | The post or comment |

Our AI provider is Coral Bricks. It does not use your data to train or improve models. Ranking also uses a small model that runs on our own servers, and your data does not leave them for that step.

## What we do not do

- **Self-identification answers are never sent to an AI model.** Wera fills those from what you saved, using rules.
- **AI never submits an application.** You press submit.
- **Scores are not shared with employers.** They only order jobs for you.
- Tailoring cannot add a new role, number or skill to your resume. It can only reorder and reword what is there.

## Limits

AI makes mistakes. A score can miss something, and a draft can sound wrong or be wrong. Check everything before you send it. A posting's text is treated as data, and Wera removes instructions aimed at AI tools before a model reads it.

## Your control

You can edit every draft, turn off AI answers and drafted answers in the extension's settings, and delete your account and the notes about you at any time.
