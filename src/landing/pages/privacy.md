Wera ("Wera", "we", "us") is a job search service at wera-jobs.com, with a Chrome extension. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- We collect what we need to find you jobs and help you apply.
- We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
- Parts of your resume and profile are sent to our AI provider so that jobs can be scored for you. Your self-identification answers are never sent to an AI model.
- You can delete your account at any time, and your data goes with it.

## What we collect

### What you give us

| Data | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account details | Name, email address, password (stored only as a hash), profile picture | To run your account |
| Resume and profile | Uploaded resume, its text, work history, education, skills, target roles, locations, the profile text you approve | To match and score jobs, and to build resumes and cover letters |
| Work authorization | Citizenship, visa status, whether you need sponsorship | To filter jobs and show sponsorship information |
| Application details | Address, phone, links, education, and answers you save for forms | To fill in applications through the extension |
| Self-identification answers (optional) | Gender, race and ethnicity, veteran status, disability status, sexual orientation, pronouns | To fill in the voluntary sections of applications, only if you choose to provide them |
| Your job activity | Saved jobs, statuses, notes, cover letters, tailored resumes, interview practice results | To run your tracker and the features you use |
| Community content | Posts, comments and reactions | To show them to other Wera users |
| Messages | What you send us for support | To help you |

### What we collect automatically

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Session and device tokens, stored as hashes | To keep you signed in |
| Last-active time | To stop spending AI credits on inactive accounts |
| AI usage and cost per account | To enforce spending limits |
| IP address, used in memory to rate-limit sign-in and other actions, and kept briefly in our hosting providers' logs | To protect the service from abuse |
| Pages visited on wera-jobs.com: the page (without anything that identifies you), the site you came from, campaign tags in the link, device type, browser, operating system, country, and how long the page was open | To count visits and improve the site |

We count visits ourselves, without cookies and without storing anything in your browser. A visitor is counted with a code made from your IP address, browser and a random value that changes every day and is then deleted, so we cannot tell who you are or follow you from one day to the next. We do not keep your IP address, link visits to your account, or share them with anyone. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted. Visit counts are deleted after 13 months. We use no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics. See the [cookie policy](/cookies).

### From the Chrome extension

On job pages, the extension sends Wera the page's address and any job data the page publishes, so it can find the job. When you use it to read a page or draft an answer, it sends the text a person can see on that page. On other pages it only checks, inside your browser, whether the page is a job page, and sends nothing. See [Chrome extension data](/extension-data).

### From public sources

We collect job postings from public job boards and use public H-1B disclosure data from the US Department of Labor. We do not store the contact or attorney names in those files.

## How we use your data

- To find, rank and score jobs for you
- To build tailored resumes, cover letters and drafted answers when you ask
- To fill in application forms when you ask
- To send account email, and digests if you turn them on
- To moderate community content
- To keep the service secure and fix problems
- To comply with the law

We do not use your data to train AI models. Our AI provider, Coral Bricks, does not use it to train or improve models either, and keeps request content for up to 30 days to operate and secure its service.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running and securing the service, your consent (for optional self-identification answers, which you can withdraw by deleting them), and legal obligations.

## Who receives your data

We share data only with the providers that run the service.

| Provider | What they do | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| Coral Bricks, our AI provider | Scores jobs, writes drafts and reviews community posts | Resume and profile text, job text, community posts, and the page text you ask it to read. Never self-identification answers |
| Vercel | Serves the website | Requests to the site, including IP addresses |
| Cloudflare | DNS and the secure connection to our servers | All traffic to our servers passes through it |
| An email provider, once email is turned on | Delivers email | Your email address and the message |

We store your data on servers we operate in the United States.

Other Wera users see your community posts, comments, reactions and display name. Employers see nothing from Wera: anything they receive comes from the application you submit yourself.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger, in which case we will tell you first.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context advertising.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, resumes, applications, letters, answers, queued and sent email, and community content. AI notes about you are erased. We keep usage and cost records without anything that identifies you, and an audit record if an admin acted on your account.

Deleted data can remain in our database backups for up to 30 days, until those backups are deleted.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and correct:** your profile, resume, details and answers can be viewed and edited in the app.
- **Export:** download your tracked jobs as CSV and your resumes as PDF or LaTeX.
- **Delete:** delete your account in Settings, or delete single answers and items.
- **Email:** turn digests off in Settings. Security messages are always sent.
- **Extension:** turn off Remember my answers in its settings, or disconnect a browser in Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or limit how we use it, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. California residents have these rights under the CCPA. To use them, email [jessechumo@gmail.com](mailto:jessechumo@gmail.com). We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Automated decisions

Wera uses automated scoring to rank jobs. The scores only order jobs for you, and you can open any job. They are not used to make decisions about you, and they are not sent to employers.

## Security

We hash passwords and session tokens, encrypt connections, and limit access to production. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires. See [Security](/security).

## Children

Wera is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

## International users

Wera is based in the United States, and we store your data there. Our providers may process it in the United States and other countries. If you use Wera from outside the United States, your data is transferred to the United States.

## Changes

We will post changes here and update the date. If a change matters, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
