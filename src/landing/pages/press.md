## Wera in brief

Wera is a job search service for students, new grads and international talent. It checks the career pages of more than 5,000 companies several times a day, scores the best matches against each person's resume, and shows which employers file H-1B visas and what they pay, using public US Department of Labor data. Wera is free during its pilot and is based in Texas.

## Data

H-1B figures on Wera come from the Department of Labor's public LCA disclosure files. Job counts change through the day as companies post and remove roles.
