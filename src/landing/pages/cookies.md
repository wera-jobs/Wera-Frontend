Wera keeps this short because we use very little. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party trackers. Our fonts and scripts are served from our own site.

## Cookies

| Name | Purpose | Lasts | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wera_session` | Keeps you signed in. `HttpOnly`, so scripts cannot read it | 30 days, or until you sign out | Strictly necessary |

## Browser storage

These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

| Key | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `wera-theme` | Your light, dark or system choice |
| `wera-session` | Whether this browser was signed in, so the app knows what to load first |
| `wera-reloaded-at` | For one tab only: when the page last reloaded itself to pick up a new version of Wera, so it never reloads twice in a row |

The Chrome extension also keeps its sign-in token, its settings and the theme in the extension's own storage.

## Your choices

Because everything here is strictly necessary or a preference you set, we do not show a cookie banner. You can clear these in your browser at any time. Clearing `wera_session` signs you out.

## Providers

Vercel and Cloudflare carry our traffic. Neither sets cookies on wera-jobs.com in normal use, but both keep short logs, including IP addresses, to protect the service.
