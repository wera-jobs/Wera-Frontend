## Why it asks for access to all sites

Jobs and application forms can be on any company's website, and forms are often embedded from another site. Wera for Chrome needs access to tell a job page from any other page on its own, and to fill forms there.

## What happens on each kind of page

| Page | What Wera does | What leaves your browser |
|---|---|---|
| Not a job page | Checks, inside the page, whether it is a job page | Nothing |
| Mail, chat, documents and feeds | Does not appear | Nothing |
| Job posting | Looks up the job in your Wera account | The page's address and any job data it publishes |
| Application form, when you fill it | Fills in your details for you to review | The form's questions, if you use AI answers |
| A page you ask Wera to read | Reads it into a job | The text a person can see on the page |
| A form you answer yourself | Remembers the answer for next time, unless you turn that off | Your answer, saved to your Wera account |
| A page saying your application went through | Marks the job as applied | The page's address |

## What it stores

A sign-in token and your settings are stored in the extension's storage. The token can be revoked in Wera's Settings. Your password is sent once, to get the token, and is not kept.

## Where data goes

Only to Wera's servers, and from there to our service providers as the [privacy policy](/privacy) describes. Self-identification answers are never sent to AI.

## Limited use

Wera's use of data received through Chrome follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including its Limited Use requirements. We use this data only to provide the features you see. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or allow people to read it, except with your permission, for security, or as the law requires.

## Your control

In the extension's settings, you can hide Wera on any site, and turn off Remember my answers, AI answers and drafted answers. You can uninstall it at any time.
