This policy applies to everything you do with Wera. Breaking it can lead to removed content, suspension or a closed account.

## You may not

- Lie on a resume or application, or use Wera to invent degrees, employers, credentials or work authorization
- Use another person's identity or resume, or impersonate anyone
- Cheat in interviews or assessments
- Share your account, or sell or transfer access
- Create several accounts to get around limits or a suspension
- Scrape Wera, resell its data, or use it to build a competing data set
- Probe, overload or break the service, except as our [security page](/security) allows
- Get around rate limits, budgets or other controls, or try to access other people's data
- Try to manipulate Wera's AI models, including with hidden instructions
- Upload malware or anything you have no right to share
- Use Wera to harass or discriminate against anyone, or for anything illegal
- Use the extension to submit applications in bulk or without reading them

## Employers and recruiters

Wera is for job seekers. Do not use it to collect candidates' data or to contact users.
